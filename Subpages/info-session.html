Information Session

Think. Calculate. Create. An introduction to our community, the people behind it, and the opportunities ahead.

What happens when a group of students brings its curiosity about mathematics into one room? At our Information Session, the Math Society introduced its community, its projects, and the many ways to take part.

The Math Society information session brings students together.

More than formulas and lectures

The session was an introduction to a society built around conversations, shared discoveries, and hands-on mathematical thinking. We presented our plans for talks, workshops, collaborations, and the Undergraduate Seminar—a place where students can explore and present ideas beyond the usual classroom setting.

Our invitation: You do not need to know everything about mathematics to take part. Questions, curiosity, and a willingness to explore are enough to begin.

A closer look at the activities and discussions during the introduction.

What the society does

Talks that connect disciplines

Our events look at the mathematics behind subjects such as artificial intelligence, art, music, and technology. They offer a chance to see how familiar ideas can be explained from a different perspective.

Undergraduate Seminar

Students can exchange ideas, share work in progress, and practise explaining a difficult subject to an interested audience. The emphasis is on learning together rather than having every answer beforehand.

Students following the Math Society presentation.

Projects and research challenges

Alongside public talks, the society aims to create opportunities to tackle problems collaboratively, try out new tools, and meet others who enjoy mathematical reasoning.

Exploring mathematics through four poster themes

The session introduced four approachable connections between mathematics and everyday life. Each theme provides a different starting point for asking deeper questions.

Mathematics behind AI

How can numbers and optimization help a model learn from examples? Linear algebra, probability, and statistics provide some of the building blocks.

Exploring mathematical ideas and society activities at the session.

Mathematics behind art

Geometry, symmetry, proportion, and patterns can shape how we make and understand visual art.

Mathematics behind music

Rhythm, ratios, frequencies, and repeating structures reveal mathematical relationships in sound.

Participants engaging with the information session.

Mathematics behind self-driving cars

Navigation combines geometry, uncertainty, and optimization: a vehicle must estimate where it is and choose how to move safely.

A moment from the Math Society gathering.

A community built through conversation

The most important part of an information session is what happens between the presentations: questions, new introductions, and the beginning of future collaborations. We hope the session made it easier for students to find a way into the society.

Conversations and shared interests at the event.
